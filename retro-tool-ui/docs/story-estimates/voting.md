# Voting

Any participant in an active session can vote on the current story while a round
is open.

## Cast a vote

During an open round, a row of point cards appears below the **Current Story**
heading. Click a card to cast your vote.

![Live session during an open round — vote cards, selected card, and participants panel](/story-estimates/voting-selected-annotated.png)

1. **Current Story** — ticket number entered at round start (e.g. `AUTH-142`).
2. **Selected vote card** — highlighted with a primary border and check-mark badge; click to vote.
3. **Participant card** — shows a vote indicator once you have voted; others cannot see the value until revealed.
4. **Reveal Votes** — facilitator-only button; enabled once at least one vote is cast.

Your selected card shows a primary-coloured border and a check-mark badge.
Other participants see only that you have voted (a check icon on your
participant card) — they cannot see your value until votes are revealed.

::: tip T-shirt scale
Sessions using a T-shirt-size template show icon cards (XS, S, M, L, XL, XXL)
instead of numeric cards.
:::

## Change your vote

Click a different card at any time while the round is open. Your vote moves to
the new card immediately.

## Remove your vote (unvote)

Click your already-selected card again to remove your vote. Your participant
card reverts to showing the dashed "not voted yet" placeholder.

## When you can't vote

Voting cards are disabled (shown at reduced opacity) when:

- **Votes are revealed** — the facilitator has clicked **Reveal Votes**. After
  reveal, only a revote (initiated by the facilitator) re-opens voting.
- **No round is active** — the session is in the _Waiting_ state before the
  first round, or between rounds. Wait for the facilitator to start the next
  round.

# Managing a poll

If you can manage a poll (its creator, or a team lead / org admin),
the card shows a **⋮** menu in the top-right corner with everything below.

![The poll actions menu, opened from the ⋮ button](/polls/manage-menu-annotated.png)

## Edit a poll

**Edit poll** reopens the create dialog with the poll's current question and
options. You can change the question, add or remove options, tweak emojis, and
toggle anonymity. **Votes on options you keep are preserved** — only removed
options lose their votes.

## Close or reopen a poll

- **Close poll** stops further voting and stamps the card with a **Closed**
  badge. Existing votes and results stay visible.
- **Reopen poll** (shown once closed) allows voting again.

Closing is handy once you've got the answer you need but want to keep the result
on the board.

## Export as PDF

**Export as PDF** downloads a selectable PDF of the poll — the question, each
option with its vote count and percentage, and the total. Good for pasting into
notes or sharing outside the team. Your browser may ask where to save it.

## Email results

**Email results…** opens a recipient picker of the poll's team members.

![Emailing poll results](/polls/email-dialog.png)

- **Select all** to email everyone, or tick specific people.
- Sending with **no one selected emails the whole team**.
- Click **Send** to deliver the current results by email.

## Delete a poll

**Delete poll** permanently removes the poll and **all its votes** after a
confirmation prompt. This can't be undone.

::: warning
Deleting is permanent — there's no history or recovery for a deleted poll. If
you just want to stop voting, **close** the poll instead.
:::

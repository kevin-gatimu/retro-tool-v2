# Results & anonymity

Results are live — every card shows each option's share of the vote as a filled
bar and a percentage, plus a total count ("N voted") and the poll's creator.

![Poll results with vote bars and percentages](/polls/vote-annotated.png)

## Public vs anonymous polls

A poll is marked **Public Poll** or **Anonymous Poll** at the top of its card —
this is chosen when the poll is [created](./creating-a-poll).

### Public polls

Voter names are visible. Hover over an option to see a tooltip listing everyone
who chose it, along with that option's vote count and percentage.

![Hovering an option shows who voted](/polls/public-voters-annotated.png)

### Anonymous polls

Only the tallies are shown — never who voted for what. There's no voter list and
no hover tooltip. Use anonymous polls for sensitive questions where you want
honest answers without attribution.

## Reading the numbers

- **Percentages** are of the total votes cast; with zero votes every option
  shows 0%.
- **"N voted"** is the number of people who have voted so far.
- Sharing results outside the app: a poll's managers can export a PDF or email
  the results — see [Managing a poll](./managing-a-poll).

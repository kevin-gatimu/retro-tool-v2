# Voting

Anyone on the poll's team can vote while the poll is open.

## Cast a vote

Click the option you want. Your choice is highlighted, and the result bars
update instantly for everyone watching.

![Casting a vote on a poll — the selected option is highlighted, with a Retract vote button](/polls/vote-annotated.png)

Each option shows a bar filled to its share of the vote and the percentage on
the right. Your own choice is outlined so you can tell it apart at a glance.

## Change your vote

Just click a different option — your vote moves to it. There's no need to
retract first.

## Retract your vote

To remove your vote entirely (and leave the poll unanswered for you), click
**Retract vote** at the bottom of the card. It appears once you've voted, while
the poll is still open (see the **Retract vote** button in the card above).

## When you can't vote

If a poll is **closed**, the options are no longer clickable and the card shows
a **Closed** badge. You can still read the final results. Only the poll's
managers can [reopen it](./managing-a-poll#close-or-reopen-a-poll).

# Responding to a survey

Open a survey from the [Surveys list](/surveys/) to see its questions (if you
haven't responded yet and it's still open) or its results (otherwise).

![Filling out a three-question survey response form](/surveys/responding-annotated.png)

1. **Text answer** — type your response.
2. **Rating** — click a star from 1 to 5.
3. **Multiple choice** — pick one option.
4. **Submit response** — answer every required question (marked with a red
   asterisk) to enable this button.

## Submitting

Click **Submit response**. You'll see a confirmation toast, and the header
switches to show **You responded**. Once you've responded (and the survey has
results to show), the page displays the results view instead of the form —
see [Results and reports](./results-and-reports).

## Editing your response

While a survey is still open, click **Edit my response** (shown under the
title once you've responded) to reopen the form pre-filled with your answers.
Changing anything and submitting again updates your response in place —
you'll see an "updated" toast rather than "submitted".

## Previewing results before you respond

If the survey already has results to show, a **View results instead** link
appears next to the form so you can peek at the summary before responding.
This is a client-side toggle only — the underlying permission to see results
is unaffected by it.

## When you can't respond

- **Closed surveys** show a message that the survey is no longer accepting
  responses, instead of the form.
- If neither a form nor results are available yet (survey open, you haven't
  responded, and there are no results to preview), the page shows "Results
  will be available once you respond or after responses come in."

# Discussion and action items

When the facilitator clicks **Move to Discuss**, the card grid is replaced by
a split-panel discussion view.

![Discussion phase: queue sidebar, focused card, and discussion notes panel](/retrospectives/retro-discussion-annotated.png)

## The discussion layout

The screen has two main areas:

- **Left sidebar** — the discussion queue. All cards are listed in
  descending vote-count order. The active card is highlighted with a blue left
  border. Cards marked as discussed appear dimmed.
- **Right panel** — the focused card at the top plus a **Discussion Notes**
  chat area below.

## Navigating cards

### Facilitator controls

Only the creator or a team lead can change the focused card. They can:

- Click any card in the sidebar to focus it.
- Use the **Prev** / **Next** buttons at the bottom of the sidebar to step
  through undiscussed cards in sequence.

The focused card is broadcast live to all participants so everyone sees the
same card highlighted.

### Marking a card as discussed

The facilitator clicks **Mark as Discussed** on the focused card. The card
gains a green **Discussed** badge and is dimmed in the sidebar. Cards are not
removed — they stay visible for the rest of the discussion and appear in the
report.

## Discussion notes

Anyone in the session can type in the **Discussion Notes** input at the bottom
right. Press **Enter** or click the send button to post a note.

Notes are attached to the card that is focused when you post them. They persist
to the report. You can delete your own notes by clicking the trash icon that
appears on hover.

## Carried-forward items

If a previous retro (on the same team) had cards that were marked for
carry-forward, they appear as a separate **Carried Forward** section at the
bottom of the sidebar, below the current retro's discussion queue.

![Discussion view showing 'Discussion Points (1)' for the current retro's cards and 'Carried Forward (2)' section for items brought from the previous retro](/retrospectives/carried-forward-discussion-annotated.png)

The facilitator can:

- Focus a carried-forward item to bring it into the main panel (it shows with
  an amber **Carried Forward** label and the text "Carried forward from retro —
  not discussed" to distinguish it from regular cards).
- Click **Mark as Done** when the item has been resolved.
- Click **Carry Forward Again** to re-forward an unresolved item to the
  _current_ retro's action items (this closes the old item and creates a new
  one on this retro).

Discussion notes can be added to carried-forward items the same way as
regular cards.

## Ending the discussion

When the facilitator is finished, they click the green **End** button in the
header. This transitions the retro to **Completed** and generates the report.

::: tip You do not have to discuss every card
The facilitator can end the session even if some cards are still undiscussed.
Those cards appear in the **Not Discussed** section of the report. The
facilitator (creator) can then carry any of them forward to the next retro
directly from the report.
:::

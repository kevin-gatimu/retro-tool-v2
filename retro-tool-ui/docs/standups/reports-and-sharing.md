# Reports and sharing

Any team member can export or email a day's standup summary from the room's
**Report** menu — it covers everyone's submissions for the date currently
open.

![Report menu open with Export as PDF and Email report options](/standups/report-menu-annotated.png)

1. **Export as PDF** — generates and downloads a PDF summary of the day
   (member updates, grouped by question) directly in your browser.
2. **Email report…** — opens a dialog to send the same summary by email.

## Emailing a summary

![Email report dialog with recipient checklist](/standups/email-report-dialog-annotated.png)

1. **Recipient checklist** — pick specific team members, or leave everyone
   unchecked to send to the whole team.
2. **Send** — the button label reflects your selection ("Send to all" or
   "Send to N").

The PDF includes the standup name, the date, and each member's answers per
question with the question's color as an accent. Emoji in answers are
stripped from the PDF (the renderer only supports Latin-1 characters) but are
preserved everywhere in the app itself.

# Submitting your update

Open a standup's room for the day you want to check in for, then click
**Add Submission** to answer that day's questions.

![Standup room with the Add Submission button and a posted update](/standups/room-overview-annotated.png)

1. **Add Submission** — opens the submission dialog for today (only shown if
   you haven't submitted yet and the day accepts submissions).
2. **A posted update** — one card per teammate, showing each answered
   question with its color accent, plus reactions and a comment count.
   Your own card additionally shows **Update** and **Delete** buttons.

## Answering questions

![Submission dialog with per-question answers](/standups/submission-dialog-annotated.png)

1. **Question prompts** — each question from the standup's setup, with a
   color-coded accent bar. Questions marked with `*` are required; answer in
   the free-text box below each one (up to 5,000 characters).
2. **Submit Update** — disabled until every required question has an
   answer.

Once submitted, your update appears as a card in the room, visible to the
whole team. You can revisit the dialog any time by clicking **Update** on
your own card — it's pre-filled with your existing answers, and saving
replaces them.

## Editing or deleting

- **Update** re-opens the same dialog, pre-filled with your current answers.
  Saving overwrites your submission for that day.
- **Delete** removes your submission for that day, along with its comments
  and reactions, after a confirmation. You can submit a new update
  afterwards.

Both actions are limited to your own submission and only while the standup
is active — see [Roles and permissions](./roles-and-permissions).

::: tip One submission per day
You can only have one submission per standup per day. Submitting again after
deleting creates a fresh one; there's no history of prior edits.
:::

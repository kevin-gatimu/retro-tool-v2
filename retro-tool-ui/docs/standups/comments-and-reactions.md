# Comments and reactions

Every submission supports emoji reactions and threaded comments, so
teammates can respond without a meeting.

## Reactions

![Reaction bar under a submission with an emoji picker open](/standups/reactions-annotated.png)

1. **Emoji picker** — click the smiley icon next to any existing reactions to
   pick from six quick emoji (👍 🎉 🔥 ❤️ 😂 👀). Existing reactions are
   grouped by emoji with a count; click one to add or remove your own
   reaction (hover to see who reacted), or pick an emoji you've already used
   to remove it.

## Comments

Click the comment count under a submission to open the comments panel.

![Comments panel open for a submission](/standups/comments-panel-annotated.png)

1. **Comment thread** — every comment, oldest first, with author, name, and
   timestamp. A delete icon appears on hover for your own comments, or on
   any comment if you can manage the standup.
2. **Composer** — write a comment (up to 1,000 characters) and click
   **Post**, or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>/<kbd>Cmd</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd>.

Comments and reactions are visible to the whole team viewing that day's
room — they aren't private replies. Deleting a submission also deletes its
comments and reactions.

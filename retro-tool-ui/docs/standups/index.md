# Standups

Standups are asynchronous daily check-ins. Instead of a synchronous meeting,
each team member submits their update whenever suits them, and the whole team
can read, comment, and react to it throughout the day.

A standup has a **cadence** (daily, weekly, fortnightly, or a one-time
check-in) and a **schedule** of which days it runs. Every day it's scheduled
for gets its own **room** — a persistent page where updates for that specific
date live. Team leads and org admins can create and manage standups; anyone
on the team can submit an update, comment, and react.

## The three screens

1. **Standup list** (`/standups`) — every standup across your teams, grouped
   by day, plus a calendar to jump to any date.
2. **Create standup** (`/standups/new`) — a manager-only form for naming the
   standup, setting its cadence/schedule, and defining the questions.
3. **Standup room** (`/standups/:id`) — a single day's page: who has
   submitted, everyone's answers, comments, reactions, and the manage menu.

::: tip Rooms are always open
The schedule sets expectations, not a hard lock — a standup's room for any
day is available to view (and, if the day is active, to submit to) whether or
not that day was actually scheduled.
:::
